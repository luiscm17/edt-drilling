No hidden defaults; errors leave no current-looking curve. Reference check: τ₀=3 Pa, K=0.2 Pa·sⁿ, n=1, γ=[0,40] s⁻¹ => τ=[3,11] Pa. At n=1 this is Bingham with plastic viscosity K; at τ₀=0 it is Power Law.
S5 (scope): Separate calculate-only UI/API, accessible model graph and tabular data; no saved cases, hydraulic coupling, Fann-fit, pressure-to-break-gel, gel strength, or operational claim. Preserve all existing study and saved-case behavior. The UI must clear stale results after parameter edits and reject malformed, duplicate, negative, nonfinite, or unrepresentable inputs/results.
